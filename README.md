# 🚀 Interactive Space Explorer

An educational and interactive website that lets you explore the solar system, discover interesting space facts, and test your knowledge with a fun multi-round quiz.

## About the Project

**Interactive Space Explorer** is a simple, beginner-friendly website designed to make learning about space fun and engaging.  
You can explore the 8 planets + Pluto, read cool facts, and challenge yourself with a 3-round quiz.

Perfect for students, space lovers, and anyone who wants to learn more about our solar system.

## Features

- 🪐 **Explore Planets** – Click on any planet (or Pluto) to see detailed information
- 🔍 **Planet Search** – Quickly find a planet by typing its name
- 🌟 **Random Space Facts** – Discover interesting facts about space with one click
- 🧠 **3-Round Quiz** – Test your knowledge with 15 questions across 3 rounds
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Works well on both computer and mobile screens
- 🎨 **Space Theme** – Dark background with stars, glowing sun, and floating moon
